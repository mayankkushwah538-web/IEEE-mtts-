About the chapter

The IEEE MTT-S Student Branch Chapter at Madhav Institute of Technology & Science, Gwalior (Deemed University) brings students together around microwave engineering, RF circuits, antennas and wireless communication.

We run hands-on workshops, expert talks and project teams so that classroom theory meets real hardware, simulation tools and industry practice.

Every branch is welcome. If you are curious about how wireless systems really work, you belong here.
